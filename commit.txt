docs(router-api): name the test that actually guards the entity (SUP-150)

The `Generation` JSDoc credited `generation.entity.spec.ts`, which exists on no
branch. The guard is `invariants.spec.ts` beside it, and it is stricter than the
comment claimed: it fails the build on an unbounded column type, on a `varchar`
longer than an identifier needs, and on a column merely named after request or
response content.

Found while writing `/privacy` for the landing page (SUP-150), which cites this
test as the reason the "we never store prompts" claim is checkable rather than
promised. A citation that points at a file nobody can open is worth less than no
citation at all.
